METERLY - PRESENTATION CHECKLIST
================================

Before opening the application
1. Copy the extracted Meterly folder to C:\xampp\htdocs\meterly.
2. Start Apache and MySQL in XAMPP.
3. For a fresh installation, create/select meterly_db and import database/meterly_db.sql.
4. If you already have meterly_db and want to keep its records, do NOT import the full dump again. Import only database/meterly_additions.sql into the existing database. It is safe to run more than once.
5. Check config/database.php if your local MySQL username or password differs from the XAMPP defaults.
6. Open http://localhost/meterly/.

Test as a customer
1. Register a new customer account with a password meeting the displayed requirements.
2. Sign out and sign in again.
3. Add a utility account.
4. Confirm the account appears after refreshing the page.
5. Confirm bill records and payment history display from MySQL when present.

Test as an administrator
1. Sign in with the existing admin account in your imported database.
2. Confirm the administrator dashboard opens automatically.
3. Load the database records.
4. Create, edit and delete a consumption record.
5. Create, edit and delete a reminder.
6. Create or update a bill and confirm its status is consistent with due date and payment records.
7. Export payment and utility-account CSV reports.
8. Confirm the activity log stores actions in MySQL.

Important
- Do not present demo data-bundle purchases or simulated receipts as real transactions.
- Do not enter real card numbers into demo checkout screens.
- Paystack requires a valid private secret key in C:\xampp\private\paystack.php and the PHP cURL extension.
- Gmail reminders require C:\xampp\private\meterly_mail.php with SMTP settings.
- Keep private credentials outside C:\xampp\htdocs.
